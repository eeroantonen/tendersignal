# TenderSignal Page Guide

## K Business Radar

- Used for: executive triage of public procurement notices.
- Used by: sales management, category leads, senior analysts, and business development.
- Benefit: converts TED/Hilma notices into prioritized Onninen, K-Rauta Pro, or joint B2B action queues.
- Next steps: validate Act now rows, inspect source documents, assign owners, and move qualified cases into CRM.

## Opportunity Map

- Used for: city-level geographic demand and value-signal review.
- Used by: regional sales leads, territory planning, category managers, and stakeholders who need the big picture quickly.
- Benefit: shows where active opportunities cluster and where public Hilma award/framework value exists without inventing exact project addresses or tender values.
- Next steps: use high-count cities to focus account review, then open the underlying notices before contacting buyers.


## Winner Lead Radar

- Used for: converting public Hilma award winners into indirect sales leads.
- Used by: Onninen sales, K-Rauta Pro sales, business development, regional sales leads, and category managers.
- Benefit: identifies contractors and suppliers that already won relevant work, then gives a source-grounded action draft without inventing private customer facts.
- Next steps: search the winner or buyer, verify the awarded scope from the source notice, and route the account to the right K business lane.

## Award & Competitor Intelligence

- Used for: public market-position review.
- Used by: commercial analysts, sales directors, account owners, and category teams.
- Benefit: separates K Group/Onninen public award signals from named competitor award signals and shows public value where Hilma provides it.
- Next steps: review competitor buyers, check renewal windows, and decide which accounts need proactive relationship work.

## Buyer 360

- Used for: single-buyer account preparation.
- Used by: named account managers, regional sales, analyst support, and bid/no-bid reviewers.
- Benefit: combines active opportunities, K/competitor public award history, public value context, and next-best actions.
- Next steps: select a buyer, review opportunities and award history, then prepare the contact plan.

## Today's Opportunities

- Used for: daily operating review.
- Used by: sales operations, inside sales, analyst duty desk, and category owners.
- Benefit: provides a repeatable morning queue with scores, lanes, recommended actions, and source links.
- Next steps: review high-fit rows, confirm deadlines/documents, and route to the correct sales or category owner.

## Category Pipeline

- Used for: category-level demand sensing.
- Used by: category management, procurement analytics, assortment planning, and commercial leadership.
- Benefit: shows demand clustering across electrical, HVAC, building materials, civil works, and tools/site equipment.
- Next steps: compare category demand with inventory, supplier campaigns, and sales focus areas.

## Notice Detail / Sales Brief

- Used for: source-grounded review of a single tender.
- Used by: account managers, bid support, sales engineers, analysts, and category specialists.
- Benefit: explains score evidence, uncertainties, source links, and produces CRM/email/checklist drafts without hallucinated facts.
- Next steps: open the source notice, inspect documents, complete the qualification checklist, and decide owner/action.

## Data Reliability

- Used for: auditability and data-quality review.
- Used by: senior analysts, data owners, interview evaluators, and anyone challenging data quality.
- Benefit: makes ingestion failures, missing fields, and source limitations visible.
- Next steps: check failed runs and field gaps, then decide whether source-specific logic or process changes are needed.

## Export

- Used for: operational handoff.
- Used by: sales operations, analysts, CRM/data users, and managers needing a portable worklist.
- Benefit: converts the filtered scored opportunity set into a CSV for Excel, CRM import, or stakeholder review.
- Next steps: create the export, review columns, and hand the filtered list to owners or downstream systems.
